# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully regardless of background, identity or experience.
Critique code and evidence rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, unwanted personal contact and publication of private information are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation after
reviewing context. Report concerns privately to the maintainer via the GitHub
contact listed in MAINTAINERS.md; do not include a person's private information
in a public issue. Maintainers should recuse themselves from reports involving
their own conduct and seek an uninvolved organization administrator.
